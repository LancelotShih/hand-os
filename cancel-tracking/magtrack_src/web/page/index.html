Magnetometer calibration: rotate the board

Keep the coil off or far away. Rotate the whole sensor assembly through every orientation while staying near one spot. The field-direction coverage must be sufficient before you can calculate the corrections. The MMC must also cover enough directions if its mounting alignment is not yet known.
