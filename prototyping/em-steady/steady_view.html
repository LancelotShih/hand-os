Earth field calibration

Learns the hard/soft-iron correction and Earth's field as a fixed vector in the reference frame. Clicking Start turns the coil off. Then slowly tumble the board through every orientation — each face up and down, and round in heading — for ~30 s, without carrying it far, and click Finish tumble. Then set it still on its mark and click Capture on mark. Use Capture on mark alone to refresh Earth's field later.

Electromagnet calibration

Hold the board still with the chip at a known offset from the magnet's centre, in the reference axes (the board's axes when it sits on its mark), at least 5 cm out. The board may be turned any way while you do this. One point is enough to start; 3–6 spread around the cube average out placement error. Redo these after a new Earth calibration.
