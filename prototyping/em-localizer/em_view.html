Electromagnet calibration

Hold the board still with the chip at a known offset from the magnet's centre, in the board's own x/y/z axes, at least 5 cm out. One point is enough to start; 3–6 spread around the cube average out placement error. The table shows how far each point solves from where you said it was.

Magnetometer calibration (hard / soft iron)

Only needed for the ahrs orientation mode. Click Start, then slowly turn the board through every orientation for ~20–30 s, then Finish. The pulsing keeps running; only coil-off samples are collected, so this works near the magnet too.
